Penafian & privasi penuh · Full disclaimer & privacy

1. Rujukan agama. Untuk hukum peribadi, sila rujuk ustaz/ustazah atau Jabatan Mufti. Falak, Imbasan Halal dan semua ciri lain hanyalah alat bantu bermaklumat. · Religious guidance. For personal rulings, consult a qualified scholar or the Mufti's office. Falak, the Halal scanner and every other feature are informational aids only.

2. Ketepatan. Waktu solat dikira dan mungkin berbeza 1–2 minit daripada jadual rasmi. Du'a, ayat dan hadith disemak dengan teliti tetapi kesilapan mungkin berlaku; sila sahkan dan laporkan pembetulan. · Accuracy. Prayer times are calculated and may differ by 1–2 minutes from the official timetable. Du'a, verses and hadith are checked with care but errors may occur; please verify and report corrections.

3. Imbasan Halal. Alat semakan berasaskan teks, bukan pensijilan halal rasmi. · Halal scanner. A text-based screening aid, not official halal certification.

4. Privasi. Tiada akaun dan tiada pelayan kami; bilangan tasbih, tetapan dan kemajuan disimpan hanya dalam pelayar anda (localStorage). Lokasi digunakan untuk waktu solat, kiblat dan masjid, dan tidak disimpan. Carian masjid menghantar koordinat kepada OpenStreetMap (Overpass), carian barcode kepada Open Food Facts, dan fon dimuat daripada Google Fonts. · Privacy. No accounts and no server of ours; tasbih counts, settings and progress stay in your browser (localStorage). Location is used for prayer times, qibla and masjids and is not stored. The masjid search sends coordinates to OpenStreetMap (Overpass), barcode lookups go to Open Food Facts, and fonts load from Google Fonts.

5. Hak cipta. Teks Al-Quran dan hadith ialah warisan umat. Reka bentuk, ilustrasi dan kod laman ini © DeenSphere. Pautan luar bukan tanggungjawab kami. · Copyright. The Quran and hadith texts belong to the ummah. This site's design, illustrations and code are © DeenSphere. We are not responsible for external links.
